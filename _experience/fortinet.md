## The Role

I'm a Project Management Intern on an 8-person team at Fortinet's Vancouver
office, working under a manager on assigned projects rather than in a
self-directed capacity. The team's day-to-day was spread across close to 100
spreadsheets that had grown organically over time &mdash; overlapping
trackers, siloed between sub-teams, with no single place to find anything.

## What I Built

The main thing I shipped was an internal search tool that unifies those ~100
spreadsheets into one place. It's used daily by my team and by multiple
partner teams, and it cut a typical information lookup from 10&ndash;20
minutes of digging through the right spreadsheet down to under 15 seconds.
It's still in active use.

Alongside that, I spent a lot of time consolidating redundant, siloed
tracking spreadsheets that different teams were keeping independently for
overlapping purposes, folding them into standardized workflows so people
weren't re-entering the same data in three places.

## Reporting

I authored weekly status reports that translated raw project data into
summaries for executive and cross-functional stakeholders &mdash; the kind of
"what actually happened this week and what does it mean" writing that sits
between the data and the decision-makers.
