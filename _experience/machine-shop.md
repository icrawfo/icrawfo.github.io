## The Role

I work in UC Irvine's undergraduate machine shop as a manufacturing
engineering intern and teaching assistant, splitting time between running the
CNC equipment, teaching, and supporting research machining requests. I'm
currently the only undergraduate certified to operate and program the shop's
HAAS CNC mill.

## Teaching

I support around 50+ students a week across two 12-person manufacturing
course sections I see every morning, plus walk-in students working on their
own projects. I played a lead role in commissioning the shop's HAAS CNC and
building out its CAM programming training, which is now part of how students
in the shop learn CAM.

## Research & Production Work

Since January 2026 I've machined parts for 15+ faculty and graduate research
projects &mdash; mostly custom brackets and test fixtures for labs that don't
have their own machining capability. On the production side, I optimized the
toolpaths on a wheel-center part for the school's EV team and cut real time
off its CNC cycle.
