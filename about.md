Ian Crawford is a Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering double-major at UC
Irvine (Class of 2028), currently serving as Chassis Lead for Anteater
Formula Racing, the school's 45-member Formula SAE team.

## What I Do

Most of my time goes to the intersection of design and manufacturing &mdash;
I run FEA validation and structural design in ANSYS Mechanical, then build
the thing myself. I'm the only undergraduate certified to run UC Irvine's
HAAS CNC mill, and I've put 150&ndash;200 hours into TIG welding steel,
stainless, and aluminum, including welding almost an entire Formula SAE
chassis by hand. That combination &mdash; owning a part from CAD through FEA
through the weld itself &mdash; is what I want to keep doing at a higher
level in industry.

## Where I'm Headed

My goal is to work in competitive motorsports design &mdash; race cars,
ideally at a team or manufacturer competing in series like IMSA. I'd also be
glad to work on the supplier side, at a company building the systems (like
suspension) that go into those cars. I'm open to general automotive as a path
into that world, but motorsports is the target.

## Beyond the Shop

I was a competitive badminton player before college. These days it's a hobby
rather than a competitive pursuit, but the discipline of training for
something that specific hasn't really left.

## Work Authorization

I'm authorized to work in the U.S. under F-1 status (CPT/OPT eligible).
